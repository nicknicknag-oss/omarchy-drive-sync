Privacy policy

This policy covers the personal Omarchy Drive Sync OAuth application operated by nicknicknag-oss.

Information accessed

When authorized, rclone accesses Google Drive file names, metadata and contents to perform the user's chosen synchronization or browsing operations. Read/write authorization can cover the entire Drive account, even though the plugin synchronizes only selected folders.

Use and storage

Drive data is transferred between Google Drive and the user's computers. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in the user's local rclone configuration. Selected files and operational logs may be stored locally. The application has no separate hosted backend operated by the maintainer.

Sharing and retention

The maintainer does not sell Drive data or use it for advertising. Google processes data under its own terms. Copies remain in the user's Drive and local storage until the user removes them; synchronization may propagate changes and deletions. Logs and local credentials must be removed separately when no longer needed.

Control and revocation

The user can pause synchronization and revoke authorization through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation does not automatically delete existing local or cloud copies.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, forms or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process visitor connection information under GitHub's privacy statement. Drive content and credentials are not uploaded to this repository or website.

Contact

Contact the maintainer through GitHub Issues. Do not post credentials or private file contents.
